Model Tenancy Act, 2021 (Central) — Section 21: Eviction and recovery of possession of premises by landlord

(1) A tenant shall not be evicted during the continuance of tenancy agreement unless otherwise agreed to in writing by the landlord and tenant, except in accordance with the provisions of sub-section (2) or in accordance with the provisions of section 22.

(2) The Rent Court may, on an application made to it by the landlord, make an order for eviction and recovery of possession of the premises on one or more of the following grounds:
(a) that the tenant does not agree to pay the rent payable under section 8;
(b) that the tenant has not paid the arrears of rent and other charges payable in full for two consecutive months, including interest for delayed payment as may be specified in the tenancy agreement, within a period of one month from the date of service of notice of demand for payment of such arrears to the landlord;
(c) that the tenant has, after the commencement of this Act, parted with the possession of whole or any part of the premises without obtaining the written consent of the landlord;
(d) that the tenant has continued to misuse the premises even after receipt of notice from the landlord to desist from such misuse;
(e) where it is necessary for the landlord to carry out any repair, construction, rebuilding, addition, alteration or demolition of the premises which is not possible without the premises being vacated;
(f) that the premises is required by the landlord for repairs, construction, rebuilding, addition, alteration or demolition, for change of use as a consequence of change of land use by the competent authority;
(g) that the tenant has given written notice to vacate and in consequence the landlord has contracted to sell the premises or taken other steps such that his interests would suffer if not put in possession;
(h) that the tenant has carried out any structural change or erected any permanent structure without the written consent of the landlord.

(3) No order for eviction on account of failure to pay rent under clause (b) of sub-section (2) shall be made if the tenant pays the landlord, or deposits with the Rent Court, the arrears of rent and other charges payable, including interest, within one month from the date of service of the demand notice.
