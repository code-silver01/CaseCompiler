Karnataka Rent Act, 1999 (Karnataka Act No. 34 of 2001) — Sections 16–17: Receipt for rent paid; deposit of rent with Controller

Section 16 (Receipt to be given for rent and other charges paid): Every tenant who makes payment of rent or other charges payable, or advance towards such rent or other charges, to his landlord shall be entitled to obtain forthwith from the landlord or his authorised agent a written receipt for the amount paid, signed by the landlord or his authorised agent. If the landlord refuses or neglects to pass such a receipt, the Controller may, on application by the tenant within two months from the date of payment, direct the landlord to pay the tenant damages not exceeding double the amount of rent or other charges paid, plus costs.

Section 17 (Deposit of rent and other charges by the tenant): Where the landlord does not accept rent and other charges tendered by the tenant within the time and manner specified, or refuses/neglects to deliver a receipt, or where there is a bonafide doubt as to the person entitled to the rent, the tenant may deposit such rent and other charges with the Controller in the prescribed manner, along with an application stating the premises, the period covered, the landlord's name and address, and the reasons for the deposit. The Controller then notifies the landlord and, on a further application, orders payment to whoever is found entitled to receive it.
